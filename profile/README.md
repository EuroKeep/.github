# EuroKeep

**Your Money. Your Data.**

EuroKeep is a self-hosted app for budgeting and money tracking. Your finances stay on your own hardware – no cloud, no third parties. Private, clear and fully under your control.

- **Accounts** – Manage as many accounts as you like, from checking to petty cash.
- **Budgeting** – Plan and stick to monthly budgets per category.
- **Tracking** – Record every income and expense in a traceable way.
- **Piggy Banks** – Create savings goals and keep an eye on your progress.
- **Analytics** – Clear reports on categories, time periods and trends.
- **Subscriptions** – Manage subscriptions and recurring payments in one place.

EuroKeep is open source (MIT) and free to use.

## Getting started

The quickest way to run EuroKeep is the Docker image [`eurokeep/eurokeep`](https://hub.docker.com/r/eurokeep/eurokeep) together with a MariaDB/MySQL database – see the [installation guide](https://eurokeep.io/install/) for details.
